# Lobby and market scene art prompts

Three images. The first two are **empty backgrounds** (no props, no characters); the third is a **props sheet** shared by both. Props are placed on the backgrounds in code, so the backgrounds must leave open space and keep clear what is walkable.

## Shared look (paste at the top of every prompt)

> 2D pixel-art in the style of Chained Echoes and 16-bit JRPGs (SNES era, modern polish), matching the warm, slightly desaturated palette and dark pixel outlines of the guild hall interior. Top-down three-quarter view (camera looking down at about 45 degrees), soft light from the upper left, no text, no UI, no characters.

## 1. Main Lobby background (outdoor guild lobby, big)

> [shared look] An **empty outdoor guild courtyard** seen from above, canvas **2048 x 1152 px** (a 32 x 18 grid of 64 px tiles). **Top 4 tile rows:** the front of the guild hall: a long stone-and-timber facade with a flat, clear wall band (no doors, windows or banners painted on it, those are added later), wooden eaves along the top edge. **Below it, the walkable plaza (about 11 tile rows):** warm light-grey cobblestone with variation, a large decorative circular mosaic of a guild crest in the centre (muted red and gold), a few worn paths and cracks, a couple of small puddles. **Edges (left, right and bottom, about 2 tiles wide):** hedges, flower beds, small trees and low stone walls that clearly read as *not walkable*. Leave the plaza free of any objects so props can be placed. Seamless on the left and right edges is not required. No props, no characters.

## 2. Marketplace background (outdoor market square)

> [shared look] An **empty outdoor market square** seen from above, canvas **1792 x 1024 px** (a 28 x 16 grid of 64 px tiles). **Top 4 tile rows:** the backs of town shopfronts: plaster and timber walls with tiled roofs, flat clear wall bands (no doors or signs, added later). **Below: the walkable market ground (about 10 tile rows):** a mix of cobbled street and packed dirt, scattered straw, a few cart-wheel ruts, a darker paved lane running left to right through the middle. **Edges (left, right, bottom, about 2 tiles wide):** low fences, hedges, barrels stacked against walls and a few trees that read as *not walkable*. Lively but uncluttered: no stalls, no crates, no characters (props are added separately).

## 3. Outdoor props sheet (shared by the lobby and the market)

> [shared look] A **sprite sheet of 36 separate objects on a flat magenta (#FF00FF) background**, canvas **1254 x 1254 px**, a **6 x 6 grid** of equal cells, one object per cell, centred, with a clear margin so nothing touches a cell edge. Each object is drawn on its own with a soft contact shadow, same scale as the guild hall props (a barrel is about 60 px wide). No text on the sheet. Objects, left to right, top to bottom:
> Row 1: fruit and vegetable market stall with a red and white striped awning; potion and herb stall with a blue and white awning; weapon and armour stall with a green awning; cloth and fabric stall with a yellow awning; small bread and cake cart; fishmonger table with ice and fish.
> Row 2: wooden notice board on legs; tall stone fountain with water; stone well with a wooden roof; guild banner on a tall pole (red with gold crest); second banner on a pole (blue with silver crest); iron lamp post with a lantern.
> Row 3: wooden bench; pair of stone planters with flowers; round tree (small, leafy); pine tree (small); wooden signpost with three arrows (blank); hitching post with a rope.
> Row 4: hay bale stack; wooden handcart with sacks; stack of three crates with apples; barrels group of three; sacks of grain pile; bird cage on a stand (empty).
> Row 5: small statue of a knight on a stone plinth; stone statue of a mage on a plinth; round stone table with two stools; wooden tent canopy (open sides, cream cloth); campfire ring with a cooking pot; stack of firewood.
> Row 6: hanging shop sign on a bracket (blank plank, shaped like a mug); hanging shop sign (blank, shaped like a sword); wooden barrel of water with a ladle; flower bed (long, mixed colours); small wooden bridge piece (short plank bridge); big guild crest standing shield on an easel.
